About This Program

Our comprehensive myofunctional therapy program for teens and adults addresses the root causes of improper breathing, swallowing, and oral posture habits. Whether you’re managing sleep-disordered breathing, supporting orthodontic treatment, or simply ready to restore proper muscle function, this program delivers lasting, meaningful change.

Program Goals

  • Correct tongue thrust and improper swallowing patterns
  • Restore proper oral resting posture for improved facial tone
  • Reduce contributing factors to sleep-disordered breathing
  • Complement and protect orthodontic and dental treatment outcomes
  • Address muscle imbalances contributing to TMJ discomfort
