About This Program

Designed for children ages 3–5 who are ready to begin building healthier oral-facial habits but aren’t quite ready for structured therapy, our For Play program meets kids exactly where they are. Through age-appropriate games, songs, and guided activities, we gently introduce the foundational exercises that support healthy muscle development — all in a fun, low-pressure environment your child will love.

Program Goals

  • Strengthen the lips, tongue, and jaw muscles through guided play
  • Support healthy facial muscle growth and tone from an early age
  • Encourage the development of proper nasal breathing habits
  • Build a positive, lasting relationship with oral-facial health
  • Prepare children for more structured programs as they grow
