Could You Benefit?

Myofunctional concerns show up in surprising ways. If any of these sound familiar, therapy may help.

Mouth breathing, snoring, or sleep apnea
Brain fog, daytime fatigue, or frequent naps
Crooked teeth even after braces
Frequent headaches or neck & shoulder pain
Attention deficit
Trouble learning & retaining information
Picky, messy, or loud eating
Thumb-sucking or nail-biting

If so, you or your family could benefit from myofunctional therapy.

The Basics

What is Myofunctional Therapy?

Myofunctional therapy is gentle, exercise-based training for the muscles of the face, mouth, and tongue. Much like physical therapy retrains your body, it retrains how your tongue rests, how you swallow, and how you breathe — so the right muscles do the right jobs.

The goal is simple: nasal breathing, lips together, and the tongue resting gently on the roof of the mouth. When those everyday patterns are in place, they support healthy jaw growth, straighter teeth, better sleep, and clearer focus — for children and adults alike.

Breathe through the noseReplacing mouth breathing with calm, consistent nasal breathing — day and night.
Proper tongue postureTraining the tongue to rest on the palate, where it guides healthy oral development.
Retrain habitsGently unwinding patterns like thumb-sucking, tongue thrust, and improper swallowing.
A coordinated teamWorking alongside your dentist, orthodontist, and ENT for lasting results.

A typical program is a series of short, easy daily exercises practiced at home, with check-ins along the way. No two plans are identical — yours starts with a free consultation.
