About Us

Myo Family Health was founded to bring a transformative new standard to oral-facial healthcare. Through evidence-based myofunctional therapy, we address the root causes of improper muscle function — helping patients of all ages breathe better, develop properly, and experience meaningful, lasting change.

Our dedicated team of physicians and specialists work together to deliver the highest quality of care, all in one place.
